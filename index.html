How the calculation works

For each item: base = guests × portion, then a spare-food buffer is added and the result is rounded up to a purchasable step (100 g / 250 g / 500 g / 1 kg) so you never under-buy.

Worked example — rice for 100 people: 100 guests × 100 g = 10 kg; +10% buffer = 11 kg; rounded up to the nearest 0.5 kg = 11.0 kg. At ₹62/kg that line costs ₹682.00.
